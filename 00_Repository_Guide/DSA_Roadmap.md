# 🗺️ My DSA Roadmap

This is my personal roadmap for learning Data Structures and Algorithms from beginner to advanced.

I am following this roadmap step by step. My goal is to build a strong foundation first and then gradually move toward advanced concepts and difficult problems.

I will not move to the next topic until I have a reasonable understanding of the current topic.

---

# 🟢 Phase 1: DSA Fundamentals

I will begin by understanding the basic concepts behind Data Structures and Algorithms.

Topics:

- Introduction to DSA
- Problem-solving approach
- Time complexity
- Space complexity
- Big O notation
- Best, average, and worst case
- Basic recursion concepts
- How to analyze a solution

Goal:

> Understand how to think about a problem before trying to solve it.

---
